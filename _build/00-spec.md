# LOCKED SPEC — 28-Module Advanced Blueprint (binding on every build step)

Everything below is locked. Work only inside it. Where anything is ambiguous, decide with best judgment inside these bounds.

## Deliverable
- A 28-module advanced business blueprint for the operator's own facial-development & bone-growth coaching business. Reader: the owner-operator only (a private playbook).
- One Markdown file per module (`modules/NN-slug.md`) plus a master index (`README.md`: a contents page linking every module and every section). No other extras (no glossary file, no appendices, no extra documents).
- Length: ~7,000 words per module (acceptable range 6,600–7,500).
- Voice: a clear hybrid of warm mentor and formal manual.
- Formatting: mixed — prose for ideas; bullets and tables for steps, comparisons, and data.

## Relationship to earlier material
- The blueprint is a SEQUEL to two earlier documents (a 20-module content & marketing program and a 10-part YouTube authority program). It must not re-teach what they already cover. Brief overlap is allowed only when it is genuinely needed as a bridge to new depth.
- Modules never mention, cite, name, or point back to those earlier documents (no "as covered in…").
- No other source material exists.
- No inherited philosophy: neither earlier document's approach wins by default. Every decision is made fresh, on merit.

## Scope of the business covered
- The whole business from first attention through the completed sale.
- Offer DESIGN is fully in scope: what each offer contains, pricing, packaging, guarantees, positioning.
- Offer DELIVERY is out of scope: running the coaching, client results, fulfilment, onboarding/retention programs.
- A FREE COMMUNITY is excluded entirely (no free Skool/Discord/group as the entry point or anywhere in the funnel). Paid offers of any type may be chosen on merit.
- Decided inside the build, on merit: the first step viewers take (replacing the free community), which paid offers exist, how sales happen, the optional extra channel, channel weighting, module grouping, optional module sections, topic breadth.

## Audience & market
- Clients: men 17–24 and men 25+. Not women; no parents segment.
- US-first English: US dollars, US rules and platform norms; still usable in the UK, Canada, and Australia.
- Business stage: agnostic — the blueprint must serve an operator at any stage.

## Channels
- All channels: YouTube (long-form + Shorts), TikTok, Instagram, X, email, podcasts & collaborations.
- At most ONE additional channel beyond these (zero is allowed), chosen in the build.
- Depth weighted by importance (weighting decided in the build).
- Paid ads: basics only.
- Platform advice: current tactics as of 2026.
- On camera: the operator shows their face from day one (personal brand). No faceless path.

## Depth & content
- "Deeper" means ALL of: more frameworks (decision tools with options and when to use each), more worked examples, more numbers (benchmarks, funnel math, formulas — expressed as ranges), more psychology (why things work).
- Balanced: every module pairs the why with concrete steps.
- New areas beyond the earlier material: operator thinking across the whole business aimed at high profit; business finance (pricing math, margins, acquisition cost, cash flow).
- NOT added as topics: AI workflows, team & hiring, other income (ad revenue, sponsorships, affiliates). Freelancers may appear as part of the operating model.
- NO facial-development science: no mechanisms, anatomy, research claims, or explanations of how facial change works. The blueprint is about the business. The brand's content topics are unrestricted.
- Standards: light guidance on how bold marketing claims can be; brief legal & platform-policy notes inside modules where they apply; brief proof systems (before/after photos, testimonials, case studies, authority signals); reputation & risk basics only.

## Operating assumptions & targets
- Solo operator + freelancers. Budget agnostic. Weekly hours agnostic.
- Analytics: the key numbers plus what to do when they're off (not a full dashboard system).
- Tools: named as recommendations only — no step-by-step setup walkthroughs.
- Business math built around $10k–$30k/month PROFIT.
- Milestones and benchmarks expressed as RANGES, never fixed targets.
- NO implementation timeline or schedule (no 90-day plans, no week-by-week calendars).

## Structure
- Suggested reading order, but every module stands alone.
- Weighted toward the business side (offers, sales, finance, operator thinking) while still covering content, channels, brand/trust, psychology, and measurement.
- Shared core template + module-specific extras. Core sections in every module: frameworks with options, worked examples, diagnostics (symptom → cause → fix), action checklist — with some flexibility. Other sections (templates & scripts, key numbers, common mistakes, key takeaways, etc.) decided in the build.
- Examples: one fictional running business across all 28 modules, plus extra examples where useful.
- Never reference real creators, competitors, or brands by name. Platforms and tools may be named.
