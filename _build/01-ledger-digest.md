## e. Gaps

In scope, yet taught by neither program. New material, researched and built fresh.

**Offer design and pricing**

- G-01 **Offer format menu** — the core design choice: 1:1, cohort, async review, hybrid and self-serve are never compared on price, capacity and margin.
- G-02 **Paid assessment as a product** — the likeliest first step has no spec: deliverable, turnaround, price band, credit toward the core offer.
- G-03 **Tiers inside an offer** — good-better-best tiers, decoys and a recommended tier lift order value; neither program designs them.
- G-04 **Offer terms and policies** — refund window, cancellation, rescheduling, plan defaults and eligibility settle disputes before they start.
- G-05 **Offer lifecycle** — knowing when to add, retire or refresh a rung keeps the ladder profitable as demand, proof and capacity change.
- G-06 **Price tests and price rises** — price is the biggest profit lever, yet no method tests it or raises it as capacity fills.
- G-07 **Platform-native paid products** — channel memberships and subscriptions could be a low-friction recurring rung; neither program weighs them.
- G-08 **International buyers** — currency display and sales tax or VAT for UK, Canadian and Australian buyers change net price and compliance.

**Sales mechanics**

- G-09 **Payment operations** — processor choice, fees, failed-payment recovery and plan collections leak revenue silently.
- G-10 **Chargebacks and disputes** — a few disputes can freeze payouts; prevention, evidence and processor thresholds are untaught.
- G-11 **Refund economics** — guarantees get chosen with no refund-rate ranges or view of their net-revenue cost.
- G-12 **Selling capacity** — call and DM hours per week, hours per sale and revenue per selling hour cap sales.
- G-13 **Minors and family payers** — 17-year-olds are in the audience: eligibility, consent, who pays and voidable contracts need brief rules.
- G-14 **Trust at checkout** — business identity, clear terms, receipts and a support contact answer "is this a scam?" at payment.
- G-15 **The sale's last step** — a confirmation that resets expectations prevents regret refunds without crossing into delivery.
- G-16 **Webinars and sales videos** — recorded or live sales presentations can sell mid-priced offers without calls; neither program weighs them.
- G-17 **Live selling** — regular lives can answer objections at scale; both programs use lives only for collaborations or launch Q&As.

**Business finance and unit economics**

- G-18 **Profit definition and owner pay** — the $10k–$30k target needs one definition: operating profit, owner pay, tax set-aside, reinvestment.
- G-19 **Budget allocation** — how much profit returns to freelancers, ads and tools sets both the growth rate and the take-home.
- G-20 **Cost benchmarks** — tools, freelancers, fees and ads as ranges of revenue expose margin problems early.
- G-21 **Paid-ads break-even math** — cost per lead and per sale against margin per offer decides whether ads ever pay.
- G-22 **Seasonality** — whether demand and cash swing seasonally (New Year, summer) is never examined; it shapes campaigns and buffers.
- G-23 **Business structure and insurance** — entity choice and professional liability cover are basic risk protection for a coach.

**Capacity and operator decisions**

- G-24 **The binding constraint** — without a way to find the one constraint across attention, conversion, offer, sales, cash and capacity, effort goes to the wrong fix.
- G-25 **Decisions under uncertainty** — a solo operator decides on thin data; rules for reversible vs irreversible bets and precommitment prevent costly swings.
- G-26 **Stop, continue or scale rules** — explicit criteria to cut or grow a channel, offer or experiment protect profit per hour.
- G-27 **Competitive response** — a plan for rivals who copy the frame, undercut the price or chase the same segment protects pricing power.

**Buyer money psychology**

- G-28 **How buyers budget** — mental accounting against gym, grooming and dental spend shapes how the price gets justified.
- G-29 **Pain of paying and financing ethics** — how paying feels (in full, in plans, through buy-now-pay-later) moves conversion and refund risk, most of all at 17–24.
- G-30 **Price as a quality signal** — in a scam-wary market, too cheap reads fake and too expensive reads predatory.

**Channels and 2026 platform realities**

- G-31 **Hosting a podcast** — an own show (audio or video) is a possible owned channel; neither program weighs it against guesting only.
- G-32 **Account safety** — policy strikes, bans and health-misinformation enforcement need a platform-loss plan.
