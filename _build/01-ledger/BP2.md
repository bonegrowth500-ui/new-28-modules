## Chunk-level insights

**Biggest deepening opportunities**
- **Sales copy for paid offers.** The chunk's copywriting is content-grade. The sales page is a bare 12-part list, "pricing and offer" and "guarantee" get one line each, and price presentation is barely taught anywhere in the source (one "payment plans" line in its objection handling). The new blueprint needs page types by offer and price, price-presentation psychology (anchoring against money already spent, per-week framing, payment plans and their refund effects, a recommended tier), guarantee wording inside the claims policy, CTA wording for each paid rung, and conversion ranges by traffic temperature (BP2-088, BP2-015, BP2-035).
- **DM and Stories selling as a real system.** Module 10 calls Instagram "the conversion platform" and DMs "where conversions happen," then offers five soft steps with no fit criteria, price moment, close, numbers or automation rules. Build qualification, branch scripts, call-versus-checkout routing, response-time and capacity rules, conversation-to-sale ranges and Story sales sequences (BP2-574, BP2-490).
- **Stories as sales assets.** "Match the story to the objection" is the chunk's most sales-relevant idea, yet four of the seven story types have no placement and client stories have no consent or typicality rules. Map story → objection → surface, add price, who-it's-not-for and referral stories, and measure close rates with and without them (BP2-170, BP2-212).
- **Money per 1,000 views.** "Views are vanity... leads are what matter" and "10,000 views, 50 leads" (5 per 1,000) are the chunk's only funnel numbers. Extend the chain to link taps, first-step conversions, sales and profit per 1,000 views by platform and video job, with the profile treated as a landing page with measured rates (BP2-425, BP2-426, BP2-436).
- **Persuasion psychology with evidence ratings.** Replace the weak or overstated mechanisms (embedded commands, neural coupling, oxytocin, "22 times more memorable," odd-number credibility) with sturdier ones and their limits: reactance and autonomy language, similarity and the negative-norm trap in social proof, reasons for small versus large asks, loss framing on real costs, and price anchoring (BP2-111, BP2-152).

**In-scope gaps the chunk implies but never teaches**
- **2026 platform facts.** The TikTok and Instagram advice predates search as a discovery route (keyword captions; Google and Bing indexing of Instagram professional accounts' posts since July 2025), Instagram's 5-hashtag cap (December 2025), 3-minute Reels, trial reels, sends per reach as a top signal, the carousel second showing, TikTok photo carousels and 4,000-character captions, comment-keyword → DM automation, and the TikTok US joint venture (January 2026) retraining the feed. Every platform number in Modules 9–10 needs restating (BP2-315, BP2-467).
- **Brief legal and platform notes.** Selling to an audience that includes 17-year-olds (Instagram Teen Accounts limit who can message under-18s; consent and payment questions), testimonial and screenshot rules, music licensing for business accounts, ad-policy limits (Meta's rules against implying a viewer's personal attributes; before/after images in ads), and no real publications or researchers named in examples ("Featured in Men's Health," the oxytocin study).
- **Operator economics behind the output advice.** The chunk stacks 1–5 TikToks a day, 4–7 Reels and 2–3 carousels a week, 5–10 Stories a day, replies within the hour and 10–15 minutes of daily engagement with no hours, cost-per-video or return logic, and never mentions freelancers or paid amplification. With a profit-centred, hours-agnostic spec, set volume from marginal return per channel (SP5-101, SP1-050) and keep paid ads to basics.
- **Proof capture versus presentation.** Module 8 teaches flattering light, camera height and a chin-up posture while calling the operator's look "a walking testimonial." The new blueprint needs a proof-capture standard kept separate from the talking-head setup (SP1-087, SP3-111) and a decision on the operator's own results as proof (SP3-316).

**Contradictions noticed**
- **The ethics rules are broken by the chunk's own examples.** Rule 1 (support every claim) versus "23 of 27," "hundreds of cases" and "500+ transformations"; rule 3 (age and genetics matter) versus "It's not your age. - **Internal number and rule conflicts.** Hook windows of 0–3 s, 0–5 s, "about 1 second" and 1–2 s; learning thresholds of 50, 100 and 50–100 videos; TikTok volume of 1–5 a day in the body versus 1–3 in the recap; 4–7 Reels a week called "daily Reels" and 6–10 posts a week called "1–2 a day"; one action per piece versus 3–5 bio links; "every video is judged on its own merit" versus "the algorithm rewards consistency"; "what works on one platform works on all three" versus Module 10's list of key differences; a full 12-part page versus "no full page for $2,000+" versus the recap's "complete persuasion architecture."
- **Conflicts with the other document, all to decide fresh.** Delivery register (20% more energy versus calm conviction, SP3-208); production priority (lighting first versus audio first, SP3-251); channel roles (Instagram as the closer versus TikTok and Reels as cold discovery, SP5-147); pillars (celebrity analysis, SP2-160); proof ranking (client stories as "the most powerful sales tool" versus proof of thinking, SP4-070); content and CTA mix (60/30/10 and 80/15/5, SP5-042); and the age band (19–28 here, 17–24 and 25+ in the spec, SP1-151).
