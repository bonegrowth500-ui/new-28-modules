**Biggest deepening opportunities**
- **The sales conversation, end to end.** Module 17 is the source's only real sales module, and it stops at one-line steps: a seven-question application with no age, budget or red-flag question; a "30-45 minute" call whose parts add up to 40–50 minutes, with no price moment and a one-sentence close; four objection categories, one of them mislabelled; no show-rate step; and a follow-up sequence with no content. Build applications, calls, objections and follow-up as one system with routing, price presentation, an answer bank for results and timeline questions, an ethics check, and show, close and follow-up ranges. Test whether calls earn the operator's time by revenue per call-hour (BP4-265, BP4-274, BP4-286, BP4-200, BP2-574, BP1-436).
- **Key numbers that end in profit.** Module 16 names acquisition cost and revenue per subscriber as business metrics, then leaves both out of its KPI list. Nothing in the chunk tracks costs, refunds, chargebacks, margin, cash, or show and close rates, and no benchmark says what to do when it's off. Rebuild the key-number set around profit: acquisition cost defined for an organic-first business, attribution that includes self-reported sources, sample-size rules, and a fix for each number (BP4-032, BP4-014, SP5-266, SP5-155, SP5-133).
- **Campaign economics and psychology.** Launches get a five-principle psychology list, a fixed calendar and a day-by-day email template, but no forecast, no fatigue budget (the template sends 10–13 emails in about two weeks), no discount policy, no waitlist mechanics and no measure beyond revenue. Decide per offer between evergreen and campaign selling, the urgency and discount policy, waitlists and pre-sales, launch frequency from fatigue signals, and a forecast and debrief in profit terms (BP4-476, BP4-481, BP4-253, BP4-501, BP4-528, BP4-604, BP4-624, SP4-374).
- **Business math with variance.** The only worked model (Module 20) targets $6,000 profit on $9,470 revenue, implying costs of about 37% of revenue that are never itemized. It puts about 79% of revenue on three coaching sales, so a month with 0 or 1 sale comes about one time in five, and it routes every low-ticket buyer through email. Rebuild it as a multi-source monthly flow model for the $10k–$30k profit range, with ranges at each step, a P&L per offer and a cash buffer for lumpy high-ticket months (BP1-463, BP1-468).
- **Operator psychology and time.** Sales avoidance gets one reframe line, selling gets a weekday slot "if scheduled," and burnout gets "push through." Protect selling time first in the time budget, and give the operator evidence to set against the fear of promoting (BP4-493, BP3-165, BP3-581).

**In-scope gaps the chunk implies but never teaches**
- **Order value and the ladder between offers.** "Minimal friction" never reaches checkout design, order bumps, upsells or payment plans (named once, inside objection handling). The two offers run as parallel funnels with a 13-fold price gap, no middle rung, and no path from $197 buyers to coaching (BP1-438, BP1-255).
- **Payment and refund economics.** Processor and platform fees, refunds, chargebacks, payment-plan defaults and the cash timing of launches and instalments appear nowhere, though the spec makes margins and cash flow core topics.
- **Brief legal and platform notes.** The chunk has no refund or payment-plan terms on the pre-launch checklist, no testimonial releases or typicality wording, no legal line on fake timers (the FTC's 2022 dark-patterns report names false countdown timers), no consent note for SMS call reminders, no Gmail and Yahoo complaint thresholds, and no rule for 17-year-olds in applications, DMs and checkout (SP4-055, SP4-086, BP3-399, BP1-121).
- **2026 accuracy.** Opens are unreliable since Apple Mail Privacy Protection. Shorts run to 3 minutes and need no #Shorts tag. Instagram reports views in place of impressions (verify), caps posts at 5 hashtags and allows shorter captions than TikTok. X analytics may need Premium, ConvertKit is now Kit and Vidyo.ai is now quso.ai (verify), and much US long-form YouTube is watched on TVs, where links can't be clicked (verify) (BP3-373, SP5-084, BP2-467, BP2-503, BP3-169).
- **Repurposing into sales assets and from collaborations.** Every repurposing flow runs from content to content: none turns objection answers, proof or clips into sales pages, pre-call packs or ad creative, and guest appearances are never clipped (SP5-370, BP3-481).

**Contradictions noticed**
- **Inside Module 20's math.** It needs about 2,500 subscribers (L6881) but targets 500–1,000 (L6889) and calls 700–1,000 enough (L7217). 2,500 subscribers at 40% opens and weekly sends give about 4,300 opens a month, not 10,000. Its 5% click rate is applied to opens, while Module 16's 2–5% "click rate" never states its base. The Month 2 milestone (about $2,000–4,000) sits far below its own goals (about $6,000–$12,000). At the plan's own margin, Month 3 revenue of $4,000–6,000+ yields about $2,500–3,800 profit, against a $6,000 profit target called "realistic within 90 days." And "2–3 new coaching clients this week" sits against 3 a month.
- **Against this source's other modules.** Posting time is a testable lever here but a myth in Module 9 (BP2-325). Promotional pushes are "quarterly or monthly" in Module 17 but quarterly in Module 19. The welcome sequence is five emails here and seven in Module 13 (BP3-330). Tweets are 3–5 a day here and 2–5 in Module 12, and application-to-call is 50% here and 60–80% in Module 4 (BP1-504).
- **Inside the chunk.** "Every single day, no matter what" clashes with "Take weekends off," and Module 19's "push through" burnout with Module 20's boundaries. "Audiences don't overlap" clashes with "repetition builds recognition." "Pillar" means proven content in Module 16 but long-form in Module 18. The weekly schedule's blocks sum to 17–22 hours, not ~20–25. The recap's "requires" hardens the body's "usually" for high-ticket calls.
- **With the other document, to decide fresh.** Ethical urgency, countdowns and "enthusiastically" repeated promotion stand against the other document's no-pressure, calm-register stance (SP4-374, SP4-173, SP4-297). Transformation stories and before/afters as standard proof stand against its proof ranking (SP4-005, SP4-070). A free lead magnet and a free challenge as entry points meet the fresh first-step decision (SP1-003). A three-stage awareness model stands against five levels plus maturity states (BP1-384). Fixed weekly and monthly review slots stand against a light, triggered rhythm (SP5-313).
