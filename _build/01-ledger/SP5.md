## Chunk-level insights

- **Biggest deepening: run the metric chain all the way to profit.** The metric stack, the decoder and the master table all stop at "community joins," which the spec excludes. Carry the chain through first-step conversion, lead to call, show, close, order value, refunds, acquisition cost, lifetime value and profit per 1,000 views, each with a range and a fix for when it's off (SP5-266, SP5-336, SP5-455, SP4-386). Fix two analytical flaws on the way. Raw drop size doesn't locate a leak, because impressions → click always drops 90% or more. And in a multiplicative funnel an equal relative lift at any stage lifts output equally, so rank fixes by headroom against each stage's range and by the cost of the fix (SP5-335). Percentages are APV, not AVD (SP5-483).
- **Short-form needs economics and attribution, not just jobs.** The chunk asserts that 3,000 long-form watches beat 200,000 TikTok views and counts "joins traceable to the Shorts funnel," but never measures either (SP5-167, SP5-227). Add self-reported attribution, tracked links and comment-keyword DMs, revenue per 1,000 views by platform, and operator hours per piece. Set volume from marginal return: 1–3 Shorts a day is a ceiling, not a rule (SP5-155, SP5-101, SP5-032, SP5-089).
- **Re-decide the whole platform map.** The chunk makes YouTube long-form the single centre and the community the place where "nurture, paid conversion, and coaching happen." The other document makes Instagram "the authority & conversion platform" and starts creators on Shorts only. Email, X, podcasts and collaborations appear in neither map here. Decide the consolidator (long-form, the email list, or both), where the sale happens, what short-form may ask for, how much trust short-form can carry, the job mix (against the other document's 60/30/10 split and 80/15/5 CTA rule), and channel expansion as capacity triggers rather than timelines (SP5-147, SP5-016, SP5-015, SP5-042, SP5-163).
- **Turn the feedback loop into a sales engine.** Replace free-community sources with sales calls, DMs, applications, email replies and refund reasons. Triage each objection (content gap, sales asset, offer redesign, disqualifier), rank objections by frequency times revenue at stake, and use objection videos as pre-call assets. "Too old" is a men-25+ objection that needs segment-specific answers with no mechanism claims (SP5-354, SP5-362, SP5-370).
- **Check everything that travels without its context.** Extend the context check and the seven-question pre-publish list to ad cuts, sales pages, testimonials (the FTC's 2024 rule, typicality disclosure, releases), emails, podcast clips, and clips cut by freelancers or auto-clip tools. The chunk never mentions paid ads; basic paid amplification of proven organic winners fits the spec and inherits these checks (SP5-117, SP5-400, SP4-055).
- **Goodhart applies to money too.** The chunk shows vanity metrics pulling toward spectacle. Close rate, cash collected and revenue per view pull the same way, toward pressure selling, overclaims and wrong-fit buyers. Pair each money metric with guardrails (refund and chargeback rates, complaints, fit rate), and make resisting a viral spike a precommitment, not an act of willpower (SP5-441, SP5-030, SP1-006).
- **Wrong-fit buyers are the paid version of the wrong viral.** "Arrive expecting the miracle you can't deliver" becomes refunds, chargebacks and bad reviews. Answer it with expectation-setting in offer copy, qualification (application questions, price as a filter, "who it's not for"), a defined serious, coachable prospect, and refund rate tracked by source (SP5-027, SP5-462, SP5-504).
- **Restate the moat as business defensibility, with its risks.** The owned list, proof library, method IP, pricing power, referral engine and search library should be valued in money. Add what the chunk omits: key-person dependence (face-first), platform dependence, evergreen decay and reputation shocks. "Patience" needs cash-flow design, so early offers fund the slow-compounding assets; the "years" language stays rhetorical, with no timelines (SP5-406).
- **Content-to-offer adjacency is implied but never built.** The chunk's best topics (progress tracking, "Why the same routine gives different results," the assessment angle) are exactly what an individualized paid offer sells. Map topics to the offer they pre-sell, use topic tests to test offer angles and price anchors, and hold the free/paid line to "honest incompleteness" (SP5-133, SP1-113, SP4-025).
- **Keep the review rhythm light, triggered, and inclusive of money.** One variant assumes a team; none reviews sales, refunds, cash or profit; and the section contradicts itself on schedules versus triggers. The spec wants key numbers plus what to do when they're off. That means a per-upload check, a periodic business review, and threshold triggers, with a decision log for business experiments (SP5-313, SP5-280).
- **2026 accuracy flags (verify at build time).** Shorts run up to 3 minutes (since October 2024), against the chunk's "sub-sixty-second" and forty-second assumptions. Shorts descriptions and comments carry no clickable links (since 2023), so the related-video link is the route. Shorts views count every start and replay (since March 2025). Also check: "most Shorts are watched muted," the wrong-viral doom loop (YouTube says Shorts don't hurt long-form), subscribers being "served" long-form, relative retention against similar videos, and "browse rarely surfaces an unknown channel to a cold audience." Native testing tools (Instagram trial Reels, YouTube Test & Compare) and shares alongside saves belong in the update (SP5-084, SP5-181).
- **Internal contradictions not to inherit.** How much trust a Short builds ("not deep trust" vs "builds belief and brand on its own"). Review "on a schedule" vs "not on the calendar." Upstream-first vs biggest-drop fix order. Model "honest" Shorts carrying unsupported numbers ("Half the time," "the single most common," "almost everyone"). "CTR rises honestly" vs "Views and CTR reward spectacle." A pipeline example whose viewer is a woman (the spec's clients are men). A "consistent with Module 11" claim that only half holds (SP5-486, SP5-487, SP5-284, SP5-077, SP5-482, SP5-498, SP5-096, SP5-162).
- **Out of scope, and names to replace.** Out: the faceless material (most of 9.3, the faceless amplifier, the faceless workflow and scaling advantage, graduation) and post-join engagement (delivery); the team review variant is rethought. Rename anything reused as a bridge: Shorts Job Selector, Discovery-to-Depth Pipeline, Repurposing Pipeline, Metric-to-Diagnosis Decoder, Master Diagnostic Table, authority leak, wrong viral, Lens A/B/C, S+/A-tier/B-tier, the seven pillars, and anything credited to the "Master Guide."
