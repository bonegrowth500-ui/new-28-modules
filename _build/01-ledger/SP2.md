## Chunk-level insights

- **Biggest deepening: put a price on "the right click."** The chunk's thesis (the right click beats the most clicks) is never quantified. The blueprint should turn it into per-video and per-angle funnel math: value per impression = CTR × qualified share × conversion rate × customer value, with CTR and retention thresholds by traffic source, and the chosen first-step conversion replacing "community joins" (SP2-152, 200, 249, 254, 256, 283).
- **Topic selection has no commercial axis.** Demand–Belief–Worldview never asks whether a topic attracts people who buy, which age segment (17–24 or 25+) it pulls, or what an upload slot costs. Add buyer/offer fit and a package-first greenlight gate (SP2-157, 080, 263).
- **The positioning spine is unused raw material for offer design.** "What must a client believe before paying," "no honest coach can promise a structural outcome," and "a trained eye beats 100 hours of content" imply a pre-purchase belief map, guarantee rules, and value-equation pricing. None of these are taught (SP2-022, 028, 034, 035, 286).
- **Architecture stops at the edge of the channel.** Roles, the Start Here path, and playlist self-selection should become one cross-channel path (Shorts, TikTok, IG, X, email, podcasts) that tags leads by state and segment and ends in the paid first step (SP2-007, 059, 087, 090, 106, 292).
- **Claim discipline has to follow the money.** Packaging-level discipline is strong. Offer names, guarantees, sales pages, ads, and calls now need one claim-intensity ladder, brief legal notes (net impression, substantiation, testimonials) and platform notes (health and body-image ad rules, before/after limits) (SP2-125, 181, 191, 267, 297).
- **In-scope legal gap: 17-year-old clients are minors.** Client before/after footage in thumbnails or ads needs written releases, with parental consent for minors, and selling to under-18s carries contract and refund risk. The chunk says only "with consent" (SP2-216).
- **In-scope gap: packaging for every other channel.** The angle selector and claim-risk ratings are YouTube-only. The blueprint needs equivalents for Shorts, TikTok, and IG covers and on-screen hooks, X openers, podcast episode titles, email subject lines, and ad creative.
- **Face-first reverses a whole layer.** Cohesion, naming, trailer, banner, and the entire thumbnail system assume a faceless start. Re-decide how the face is used in thumbnails (expression range without guru coding), personal-name vs method-brand naming, and whether Story/Personal returns as a pillar (SP2-014, 044, 114–116, 208, 212).
- **Contradictions with the other document.** Celebrity analysis is endorsed there and banned here. Arrows and circles on features, emotional faces, and before/after split thumbnails are endorsed there and restricted here. The documents also clash on doom words ("Ruin Your Progress"), on a fixed 60/30/10 mix vs a library-gap mix, on 4–6 pillars vs four at most, and on a single medical-establishment enemy vs two poles (SP2-018, 044, 053, 079, 160, 215, 228–231).
- **Internal contradictions to resolve, not inherit.** The chunk says both "about five" and "five to nine" title words. It praises "in 30 days" and later bans it. Its "The real reason" template collides with its own single-cause trap, and its own case study breaks its one-variable rule. It lists five trust questions but tests four, specifies only 7 of 9 build slots, and labels "Stalled After 6 Months" an Assessment title although it has the Curiosity-Gap form (SP2-083, 130, 153, 187, 223, 260, 296).
- **2026 accuracy flags.** Four claims need verifying and restating as ranges: "YouTube rewards session time," "search is the most reliable cold start," manual swap-testing (native Test & Compare now exists and judges by watch time), and "videos per session" (not a Studio metric) (SP2-011, 076, 131, 247).
- **Diagnostics without thresholds.** "Low videos-per-session," "healthy impressions," "healthy CTR," "enough impressions," and "the first window" are never defined. The spec requires key numbers as ranges plus what to do when they're off (SP2-131, 134, 254, 257, 276).
- **One way to find the constraint, not two fix orders.** 3.11 says fix the spine before touching thumbnails; 4.12 says run the packaging autopsy first because it's cheapest. The operator layer needs one business-wide decision tree for finding the binding constraint (SP2-137, 275).
- **Audience quality is also sales risk.** Desperate viewers pulled in by fear are treated only as a bad content audience. For a paid-offer business they are refund, chargeback, and reputation risk, so qualification belongs in the sales design (SP2-294).
- **Coin fresh vocabulary.** The signature terms (S+, Two-Job Scorecard, Start Here spine, click contract, Demand–Belief–Worldview, packaging angle selector) would read as pointers back to the earlier program. Rename any concept reused as a bridge.
